What is Resinate?

Small-batch resin art, poured for you

A piece of resin that's yours alone: your colours, your design, set in glass-clear resin and poured by hand.

Resinate is a small handmade resin studio. We mix epoxy and UV resin, tint it, layer in pigment, sparkle and real inclusions, then let it cure into a solid, glossy piece you can hold up to the light.

Nothing sits on a shelf waiting for you. Every piece is poured after you order it, so you choose the size, the colours and the finish. Want your own picture or a 3D design set inside? A wave with real sand at the bottom, or leaves from somewhere that matters to you? That's what we do.

Because resin moves while it sets, no two pieces ever come out the same. The swirls, bubbles and shimmer in yours won't exist anywhere else.

Will my piece look exactly like the photos?

Close, but not exactly. Resin moves while it sets, so the swirls, bubbles and sparkle in every piece come out a little different. That's what makes yours one of a kind.

Can I use my own picture or design?

Yes. Pick "Your own design" in the builder and send us the image, or describe the 3D design you want. Our themed designs cost nothing extra; your own design adds a small charge. Add a note when you order and we'll be in touch.

What can go inside a piece?

Glitter, mica and flakes, plus custom inclusions like crystals, sand, dried leaves or small scenery. Tell us your idea and we'll quote it before we start.

How do I pay?

By PayID or bank transfer using your order reference, or with cash or card at our market stall. Custom work gets a confirmed price before you pay.

How long does an order take?

Each piece is poured and left to cure after you order, so it takes a little time. We'll let you know when you order, and you can check progress any time under Track order.

How do I look after my piece?

Wipe it with a soft, damp cloth. Keep it away from high heat, and out of strong direct sun for long periods, which can slowly yellow resin over time.
