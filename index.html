What is Resinate?

Small-batch resin art, poured for you

A piece of resin that's yours alone: your colours, your design, set in glass-clear resin and poured by hand.

Resinate is a small handmade resin studio. We mix epoxy and UV resin, tint it, layer in pigment, sparkle and real inclusions, then let it cure into a solid, glossy piece you can hold up to the light.

Nothing sits on a shelf waiting for you. Every piece is poured after you order it, so you choose the size, the colours and the finish. Want your own picture or a 3D design set inside? A wave with real sand at the bottom, or leaves from somewhere that matters to you? That's what we do.

Because resin moves while it sets, no two pieces ever come out the same. The swirls, bubbles and shimmer in yours won't exist anywhere else.

Questions?

Message us

Ask about a custom piece, an order or anything else. Your message goes straight to the Resinate team, and we'll reply by the phone, email or Instagram you give us.

Will my piece look exactly like the photos?

Close, but not exactly. Resin moves while it sets, so the swirls, bubbles and sparkle in every piece come out a little different. That's what makes yours one of a kind.

Can I use my own picture or design?

Yes. Pick "Your own design" in the builder, sign in, and upload your picture or add words; you'll see it on the preview. Or describe the 3D design you want. Our themed designs add a little to the price, and your own design a bit more.

What can go inside a piece?

Glitter, mica and flakes, plus custom inclusions like crystals, sand, dried leaves or small scenery. Tell us your idea and we'll quote it before we start.

How do I pay?

By PayID or bank transfer using your order reference, or with cash or card at our market stall. Custom work gets a confirmed price before you pay.

How long does an order take?

Each piece is poured and left to cure after you order, so it takes a little time. We'll let you know when you order, and you can check progress any time under Track order.

Where does the money go?

After materials are paid for, all of the profit goes to charity. See privacy & regulations.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of buying. Message us with your order reference. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law apply too; see privacy & regulations.

How do I look after my piece?

Wipe it with a soft, damp cloth. Keep it away from high heat, and out of strong direct sun for long periods, which can slowly yellow resin over time.

    Privacy & regulations

    How we look after you

    Where your money goes, how refunds work, and what we do with your details. Last updated October 2026.

    All profit goes to charity

    After we cover the cost of materials (resin, pigments, moulds, LEDs and packaging), all of the profit from every sale goes to charity.

    Ask us at the stall or through our contact details if you'd like to know which charity your order is supporting.

    Refunds

    • You can ask for a refund within 7 days of buying. The 7 days start on the day you pay.
    • To ask, contact us with your order reference (it looks like RSN-AB12C). If you've already collected the piece, please return it to us.
    • We refund the same way you paid: PayID or bank transfer back to you, or cash if you paid cash.

    This doesn't take away any of your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If a piece is faulty, isn't what we described, or doesn't do what it should, you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund, even after 7 days.

    Privacy policy

    What we collect. Only what we need to make and hand over your order:

    • Your name, and the phone number, email or Instagram you give us.
    • What you order, your notes, and any picture or words you upload for your own design.
    • Messages you send us through the "Message us" form.
    • If you make an account: your email address, and the name, contact and profile photo you choose to add.
    • Payments you make, so we can match them to your order. We never see or store card or bank login details: you pay by PayID, bank transfer or cash.

    How we use it. To make your order, confirm the price, tell you when it's ready, and keep track of payments. We don't use it for advertising, we don't send marketing, and we never sell or share your details with anyone else.

    Who can see it. Only the Resinate makers who handle orders. Other customers can't see your orders or details. To check an order on this site, someone needs both its reference and the contact you ordered with.

    Where it's kept. In our database with Supabase, which encrypts the data it stores. Everything sent between your browser and us is encrypted (HTTPS). The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts; like any website host, they can see your IP address when you visit.

    On your device. This site saves your unfinished order, your settings and (if you choose) your past order references in your browser's storage. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear it any time in Settings.

    Your pictures. Pictures you upload for a design are given a random, hard-to-guess link so we can print them. Don't upload anything you wouldn't want us to see.

    Keeping and deleting. We keep order records so we can handle refunds and keep our charity accounts straight. You can delete your account in Settings, which removes your profile and photo. To have your past orders removed or corrected too, contact us and we'll do it.

    Questions or complaints. Contact us through the details on this site or at the stall. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
